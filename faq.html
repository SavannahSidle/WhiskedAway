The useful fine print

Questions,
answered.

Clear expectations make better cake. They also prevent 11:48 p.m. messages about a seven-tier wedding cake needed tomorrow.

Where is pickup?

Pickup is by appointment from a fictional home studio in the Manotick area. The exact address is provided only after the order is confirmed. There is no storefront or walk-in service.

How much notice is required?

Standard menu items need at least four days’ notice. Custom cakes usually need 14 days. Larger event orders may need four to six weeks.

Can I place a rush order?

Sometimes. Orders inside the normal notice period depend on availability and may include a 20% rush fee. A rush request is not confirmed until accepted and paid.

Can you copy an inspiration photo exactly?

No. Inspiration images help communicate colour, mood, and technique. Every cake is made by hand and will be an original interpretation, not an exact replica of another baker’s work.

Can you accommodate allergies?

The studio handles wheat, eggs, milk, soy, peanuts, and tree nuts. Dietary options may be available, but the kitchen is not allergen-free and cannot guarantee against cross-contact. Customers with severe allergies should not order.

What happens if I am late?

Pickup windows are scheduled. Contact the baker as soon as possible if delayed. Orders more than 30 minutes late may need to be collected during the next available window. Missed pickups are not refundable.

Policies at a glance

Before the mixer starts.

Standard orders

Paid in full when confirmed. Cancellation more than seven days before pickup receives a refund minus payment-processing fees. Later cancellations are non-refundable.

Custom orders

A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves the date. The balance is due seven days before pickup. The order may be cancelled if the balance is unpaid.

Changes

Small changes may be accepted before final approval. Major changes to size, flavour, or design can require a revised quote and may not be possible close to pickup.

Design variation

Colour, flowers, piping, and placement vary naturally. Reasonable artistic variation is not grounds for a refund.

Quality concerns

Contact the baker within 24 hours of pickup with clear photographs. Refunds or credits are considered based on the specific issue. Change of mind, missed pickup, and preference-based concerns are not refundable.

For the future real business

How payment would work

Recommended: Stripe Checkout, opened from this site through a tiny serverless function. Stripe would securely collect card details, send payment confirmation, and return the customer to an order-confirmation page. Secret keys would live in the serverless service, never GitHub.

Stripe’s Canadian standard rate is currently 2.9% + CA$0.30 per successful domestic card transaction, with no setup or monthly fee for standard payments. Stripe Checkout is included. A custom checkout domain is optional at US$10 per month. Check current Stripe pricing.

Alternative: Square Online Checkout, useful if the baker also uses Square for in-person sales. Its Canadian online rate is currently 3.3% + CA$0.15 per transaction, with extra charges for international cards. Check current Square pricing.

Before activation, the baker must provide the legal business name, contact details, bank account for payouts, tax settings, refund policy, product catalogue, pickup schedule, order-notification email, and a verified Stripe or Square account. This portfolio checkout does not process money.
