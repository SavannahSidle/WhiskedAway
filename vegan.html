Important kitchen note

Vegan does not mean allergen-free.

The studio also handles wheat, eggs, milk, soy, peanuts, and tree nuts. Ingredients for each bake are selected without animal products, but shared equipment and surfaces mean cross-contact cannot be ruled out.
